Costs

What it costs to run. Where your $5.99 goes.

sealctl is run by one developer on standard services. These are the running costs of the public version, in US dollars, updated when they change. Contracts with organisations (Teams, Enterprise) are separate and not shown here.

The relay costs the same whether a hundred or a few thousand phones use it: it carries small sealed messages and stores almost nothing. Development time is not counted here.
