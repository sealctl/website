Pair a machine with your phone

This link comes from sealctl pair on a computer. It opens the sealctl app on a phone that has it installed.

If you are reading this on your phone, the app is not installed yet, or your browser opened the link instead. In the app, go to Machines, tap Pair a machine and scan the code that sealctl pair shows.

The part of the link after # stays in your browser: it is never sent to this site.
