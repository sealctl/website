Pair a machine with your phone

This link comes from sealctl pair on a computer. Opened on that computer, this page shows the pairing code: on your phone, open sealctl, go to Machines, tap Pair a machine and scan it.

On a phone, the link opens the app's scanner. Pairing needs the app's own camera, which shows the phone is next to the computer.

The part of the link after # stays in this browser: it is never sent to this site.
