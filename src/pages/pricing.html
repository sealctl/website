Pricing

Free to use. $5.99 once if it earns its place.

Run sealctl buy and pay once through Paddle's checkout. No subscription.

What is free

The app, the command line tool and the relay. Nothing in the app changes when you pay.

When you're asked

After 30 days with use, one line in your terminal after a command you typed, at most once a week. Never in sealctl run output, pipes, CI or anything an agent reads. Never on the phone.

Who can pay

You, by card; your company, with a Paddle invoice and its VAT ID; or anyone you send a payment link to. sealctl buy asks which.

Teams

sealctl buy --seats 10 gives one code; each developer runs sealctl buy --code XXXX-XXXX. A claimed seat stays with that person.

One licence, all your machines

It belongs to your phone's identity, survives a restore from the words, and moves with you when you change words.

Taxes and currency

The price is in US dollars. VAT or sales tax depends on your country and is shown at checkout.

Payments by Paddle

Paddle is our reseller and merchant of record. They handle checkout, VAT, invoices and refunds. We never see your card. Refunds: see Refunds.

Questions

Will the app ever ask me to pay?

No.

What if I never pay?

It keeps working.

Does paying change limits?

No. Everyone has the same 5,000 requests a day per phone, which normal use never reaches.
