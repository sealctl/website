2. The short version

No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising. The website sets no cookies. The relay stores public keys, machine names and an encrypted backup it cannot open. The relay's code stores and logs no IP address. If you give feedback or say you would buy in sealctl buy, it keeps those answers with your phone's id, never a name or email.

3. The website

The website is served by Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses to deliver pages and stop attacks; we get no logs of visitors. The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

The exception is the payment page (/pay), which loads Paddle.js. Paddle sets the cookies and storage it needs for checkout and fraud checks.

If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser remembers it on your device. It is never sent to us.

The status page asks the relay whether it is up, directly from your browser.

Your location never leaves the phone in readable form: places travel only inside the encrypted backup. The relay never learns which key was used, which command ran or what the agent did.

Cloudflare, which runs the relay, sees network addresses to deliver traffic and keeps the addresses of requests it blocks as attacks in its security log. Operational logs hold no keys, addresses or identifiers and are kept 7 days; daily counts with no identifier are kept 3 months. A phone that makes no call for 2 years has its data deleted; a licence record and its usage count stay.

Feedback and "I'd buy it"

sealctl buy lets you give feedback instead of paying: four questions, for a year's licence. Where its menu shows "I'd buy it", you can also say that you would buy. Both are your choice, and neither asks for a name or email. The relay keeps what you send with your phone's id and the time: your answers (what you use sealctl for, whether the price is fair, what is missing, and one optional line of at most 280 characters), or that you would buy. Only we read them, on a private admin page.

Deleting your relay data deletes the answers and the "I'd buy it" record. The record that a free year was given, and until when, stays, as a bought licence record does: a phone restored from your words keeps the rest of its year and is not given a second one. Ask us to erase that record too.

5. Payments

Paddle is the merchant of record and a separate controller for payment data; see Paddle's privacy notice. Paddle shares with us what we need to support you and keep tax records: name, email, country, company name and VAT ID if given, and what was bought. We never see card details.

To link your payment to your phone, we pass your phone's public identity key to Paddle, which keeps it with the purchase. Deleting your relay data doesn't delete Paddle's records.

6. Email

If you write to us, we keep the conversation to answer you and for {{legal.emailRetention}}.

7. App stores and push

Apple and Google distribute the app under their own privacy terms. Push notifications go through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging; they carry no request content. On Android, Firebase also creates an installation id that Google uses to deliver notifications.

8. Processors

Cloudflare (website, relay hosting, email forwarding), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service (notifications), {{legal.emailProvider}} (email). Apple and Google distribute the app as independent controllers under their own terms. Paddle is a separate controller as merchant of record. GitHub's privacy statement covers issues and security advisories on GitHub. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it does so under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

9. Legal bases

  • Contract: the relay service and the licence, including a free year for feedback.
  • Legal obligation: tax records.
  • Legitimate interest: delivering and securing the website and relay, the abuse limit, the request to pay and anonymous daily counts, learning from the feedback and "I'd buy it" answers you choose to send, answering email.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, and to object. You can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or to the authority in your own country.

To delete your relay data: in the app, Settings, Delete relay data and unpair, or Unseal all machines and leave (when available); or write to {{email.privacy}} with your phone's identity fingerprint. Machines unpair from the phone or with sealctl unpair.
