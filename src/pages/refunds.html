Refunds

Draft for launch. The refund window is still to be confirmed.

Ask within [30 — to be confirmed] days of purchase, for any reason, and you get your money back. Write to {{email.support}} with your Paddle order number, or use the link in Paddle's receipt email. Paddle issues the refund to the original payment method.

EU consumers

You also have the 14-day right of withdrawal as stated in Paddle's buyer terms.

Team seats

Refunds for team seats can be partial, for seats nobody has claimed. Claimed seats stay with their people.

After a refund

A refunded licence goes back to unpaid. sealctl keeps working; only the weekly request to pay in your terminal comes back.
